============================================================================================
SMARTSME DEMONSTRATION DATASET: TWO COMPARABLE PROVISION STORES IN BENGALURU
============================================================================================

Period: 15 July 2026 to 11 November 2026 (120 days).
  Days 1-90, 15 July 2026 to 12 October 2026: baseline history.
  Days 91-120, 13 October 2026 to 11 November 2026: demonstration period.

Both shops are wholesale-and-retail provision stores in West Bengaluru that supply darshinis,
restaurants, PG kitchens, a bakery and a small retailer on credit, and sell to walk-in
customers at the counter. They carry the same 24 products, start with the same opening stock,
pay the same rent and serve the same mix of customers. Their outcomes differ because of how
each is run.

HOW TO ENTER IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Create each business under its own owner account and do its Setup section first.
2. Then, in this order: purchases, sales, expenses, customer payments, supplier payments, and
   finally cancellations and write-offs. Entering every purchase before any sale is safe:
   stock only ever runs higher than it did on the day, and invoice numbers come out in date order.
3. Purchases, sales and expenses go into Smart Input one sentence at a time. Each sentence names
   its own date; check the Date field on the confirm screen shows it.
4. "paid ₹X/-" in a sale or purchase sentence means type X into Amount paid. Without it, leave
   Amount paid at 0 and the document stays on credit until its payment line further down.
5. Payments, cancellations, write-offs and the deadline change are not Smart Input notes:
   each line says which page and button to use.
6. The health score is worked out from the day it is viewed. The figures in sections 7 are for
   11 November 2026. Viewed a few days later they drift down a little for both shops, because the
   last-30-days window starts to include days with nothing recorded.
7. Typed notes work with or without an AI key. The whole dataset was loaded into a copy of
   SmartSME with no AI key (offline parser): every note produced the intended draft, every
   payment was accepted, and the dashboard matched section 7. With a Gemini key, the free tier
   allows about 15 notes a minute.

============================================================================================
BUSINESS A: SRI ANNAPOORNESHWARI PROVISION STORES, RAJAJINAGAR, BENGALURU
Scenario: struggling business
============================================================================================

Starts the period slightly larger than Business B, then loses ground. It buys in small lots at
higher prices, discounts often to hold customers, gives easy credit and collects late, pays its
own suppliers late, and runs short of fast-moving stock. A festive-season bulk buy of cashew,
basmati and ghee ties up cash. Ganga Veg Restaurant leaves after repeated shortages (one
delivery is rejected and cancelled), Royal Comforts PG stops ordering, and from late October the
rice, oil and wholesale suppliers stop supplying until old bills are cleared. A third staff
member, a cable TV advert, chiller repairs and a moneylender's interest push costs up.
